## Sample Answers

### "How does SAFAR work?"
SAFAR has two user types:

• Drivers: Post available seats on upcoming trips
• Passengers: Browse rides on map or list view, send join requests

Once a request is accepted, both parties can chat to coordinate pickup/drop-off details. SAFAR handles coordination — payment happens offline between riders.

### "How do I post a ride?"
1. Tap the '+' button on the home screen
2. Select origin and destination cities
3. Set departure date and time
4. Enter number of available seats
5. Set price per seat (optional)
6. Tap 'Post Ride'

Your ride will appear on the map for nearby passengers to discover.

### "Is SAFAR safe?"
SAFAR takes safety seriously:

• All users verified via phone number
• Driver/passenger ratings visible
• In-app reporting system
• Safety guidelines provided

However, always trust your instincts. Share trip details with someone you trust and meet in public places when possible.

### "How does payment work?"
SAFAR does not handle payments. The app is for coordination only. Drivers and passengers arrange payment offline, typically:

• Cash on the ride (most common)
• Mobile wallet transfer (JazzCash, Easypaisa)
• Bank transfer (for regular carpoolers)

Agree on the method before the trip starts.
